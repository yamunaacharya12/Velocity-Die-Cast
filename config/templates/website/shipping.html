Shipping Information

We ship worldwide from our fulfillment centers via tracked courier. Orders placed before 2PM local time ship the same business day.

Standard Shipping

3–7 business days domestic, 7–14 business days international. Free on orders over $100.

Express Shipping

1–3 business days domestic, flat rate $19.99.

Packaging

Every model ships in a rigid protective mailer with internal foam bracing to prevent shifting or paint damage in transit.
