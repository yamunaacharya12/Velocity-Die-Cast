About Velocity

Velocity Die-Cast Co. was founded by a small team of motorsport engineers and lifelong collectors who felt the hobby deserved better than mass-market toy-aisle finishes. Every model in our catalog goes through a six-stage tooling process, hand-inspected paint QC, and individually protected packaging before it ever reaches a shelf.

We work directly with licensing partners across the supercar, hypercar, and motorsport world to reproduce vehicles at true scale — from a pocket-sized 1:64 daily driver to a museum-grade 1:18 centerpiece.

Our Promise

Authentic tooling, verified materials, and a collector community of thousands who trust us with their shelves.

Where We're Headed

We're expanding our Limited Edition program with numbered, individually certified releases, and rolling out a members' early-access program for our most dedicated collectors.
